RiverCast

Bacteria levels across Norwich's rivers during the storm of 5 to 9 July 2024, and what a graph neural network forecast for each hour, 6 hours in advance.

Truth Graph WaveNet (shaded: 80% range) LSTM Alert level

What you are seeing

  • Each circle is a 2.5 km stretch of river. Darker means more faecal bacteria. An orange ring means it is above 1,000 CFU per 100 mL, the usual alert level. Dashed circles have no sensor.
  • Truth is simulated from the real rain and river flow of that week.
  • Graph WaveNet knows how the rivers connect. The LSTM treats each stretch on its own. Both forecast 6 hours ahead and never saw this storm in training.
  • The chart below the map follows one stretch over the four days. Click any circle to change it.

Over July to December 2024, Graph WaveNet's error was 31% lower than the LSTM's at stretches without a sensor.

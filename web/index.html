RiverCast · live demo

Watch a storm move through Norwich's rivers

RiverCast is a research prototype that forecasts river pollution a few hours ahead. A graph neural network (Graph WaveNet) knows how the river is connected, so it can follow pollution downstream, even to places with no sensor.

How it works

A real river graph

The Yare, Wensum, Tud and Tas from OpenStreetMap, cut into 31 reaches of about 2.5 km and linked in the direction the water flows.

Simulated pollution

Real rain and river flow drive a simulator: sewer overflows, treatment works and farm runoff add bacteria, norovirus and AMR genes, which flow downstream and decay in sunlight.

Graph WaveNet

Time convolutions read the last 24 hours, and graph convolutions pass information along the river. One model forecasts all three pollutants, with an uncertainty band.
